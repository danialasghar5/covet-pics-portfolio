## 📌 Project Overview

CovetPics is a Shopify-focused visual commerce and User-Generated Content (UGC) platform that helps merchants transform customer photos and videos into engaging, shoppable storefront experiences.

The platform enables merchants to collect, manage, and showcase visual content from Instagram and direct customer uploads. It combines interactive product tagging, customizable galleries, photo reviews, creator usage rights management, analytics, and subscription billing into a unified commerce solution.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

- **Gallery Management**
  - Standard, product-specific, and customer-generated galleries.
  - Responsive grids, sliders, carousels, and collages.
  - Customizable gallery layouts and styling.
  - Drag-and-drop media organization and bulk sorting.

- **Instagram & Media Integration**
  - Instagram Graph API integration.
  - Automated hashtag-based media synchronization.
  - Direct photo and video uploads.
  - Image compression and content moderation.

- **Shoppable Commerce**
  - Shopify product tagging on images and videos.
  - Interactive product hotspots.
  - Multi-product tagging and quick-view experiences.
  - Product catalog synchronization.

- **Photo Reviews & Customer Engagement**
  - Automated post-purchase review requests.
  - Customer photo and testimonial submission widgets.
  - Review imports from Loox, Yotpo, and Judge.me.
  - Review management and CSV exports.

- **UGC Usage Rights Management**
  - Creator and customer permission requests.
  - Secure, tokenized permission URLs.
  - Branded approval and rejection workflows.
  - Permission status tracking.

- **Analytics & Billing**
  - Gallery impressions and engagement tracking.
  - Product interaction and attributed sales analytics.
  - Shopify recurring billing and Chargebee integration.

## 🏗️ Application Architecture

CovetPics follows a modular Ruby on Rails architecture with dedicated components for merchant management, media processing, Shopify integration, and storefront experiences.

- **Application Layer:** Ruby on Rails with modular controllers, models, and services.
- **Frontend Layer:** Hotwire Turbo and Stimulus.js for reactive user interfaces.
- **Data Layer:** PostgreSQL for relational data and Redis for caching and background job processing.
- **Background Processing:** Sidekiq workers for asynchronous tasks and media synchronization.
- **Storefront Integration:** Shopify Online Store 2.0 Theme App Extensions and Liquid app blocks.
- **Media Infrastructure:** AWS S3 storage and Cloudflare-based image delivery.
